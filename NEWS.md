# AI Browser news

This build has no chat. Release notes are in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## 1.0.2 — 2026-11-21

- The AI Browser menu in the status bar has **Get the full version — HoverDeck**, a link to HoverDeck, which adds a chat for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and more. It is hidden once HoverDeck is installed.

## 0.10.0 — 2026-11-14

- The AI Browser chat moved to its own extension, [HoverDeck](https://open-vsx.org/extension/DenysDavydov/hoverdeck). AI Browser keeps the browser, its MCP tools and Connect for your assistants.
